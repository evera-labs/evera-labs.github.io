1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to GTA All Cheats ("the Application", "our App"), an offline companion guide published by Evera Labs ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") to provide gamers with quick access to cheat codes, vehicle spawns, and gameplay button sequences across various Grand Theft Auto titles.

We firmly believe in privacy by design. Our core philosophy is straightforward: you should be able to enjoy your games without sacrificing your personal information. GTA All Cheats does not require an account, does not ask for personal identity documents, and operates without transmitting your data across the internet.

Zero Tracking Guarantee: GTA All Cheats does not track your location, does not scan your contacts or photos, and has zero advertising SDKs or analytics trackers embedded in its binaries.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To eliminate any ambiguity, we explicitly state that we do not collect, process, record, or share any of the following categories of data:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): Full names, residential addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or social security numbers.
  • Financial Data: Credit card numbers, bank accounts, billing records, or Google Play purchase histories.
  • Device & Telemetry Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID/IDFA), IMEI, MAC address, IMSI, or hardware serial numbers.
  • Location Data: Fine (GPS) or coarse (network-based) geographic locations.
  • Media & Files: Photos, videos, audio recordings, or personal documents stored on your device.
  • Gaming Accounts: Rockstar Games Social Club credentials, PlayStation Network IDs, Xbox Live gamertags, or Steam account tokens.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To offer a rich, tailored experience, the Application preserves certain non-personal configuration preferences locally on your physical device using Android's built-in Room SQLite Database and SharedPreferences.

4. Device Permissions Explained

The Application has been deliberately engineered to minimize all permission footprints:

  • No Runtime Permissions: The App does not request camera, microphone, location, contact, phone state, or storage permissions at runtime.
  • No Network Permission: All cheat codes, descriptions, button sequences, and game categories are bundled directly within the app's compressed local assets. The app does not require active Wi-Fi or Cellular data to display any cheat code.

6. Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Protecting the digital privacy of children is of paramount importance. GTA All Cheats is designed as a family-safe utility that strictly complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

Because our application does not collect, retain, or transmit any personal data whatsoever, no personal data from minors or children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area) is ever gathered.

7. Data Purging & User Control

Since all preferences and bookmarks remain strictly on your Android device, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  1. In-App Unfavorite: You can remove any cheat code from your Favorites list anytime by simply toggling the heart icon off.
  2. Clear Storage via Android Settings: You can completely reset all favorites, platforms, and onboarding answers by opening Android Settings > Apps > GTA All Cheats > Storage > Clear Data.
  3. Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application completely removes the application's private sandbox directory, permanently wiping all local database entries.

8. Security Architecture

Because GTA All Cheats operates in an offline posture with zero cloud infrastructure, the customary security vulnerabilities associated with cloud databases (such as data breaches, unauthorized server access, SQL injections, and network interception) are systematically mitigated.

Local data is protected by the Android Operating System's application sandboxing model, isolating the Application's files from unauthorized third-party apps.
