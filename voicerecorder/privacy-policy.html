1. Introduction

Welcome to Voice Recorder: Audio Recorder (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data practices.

This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles your information, what permissions we require, how third-party services operate within the App, and your rights under global privacy laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

KEY PRIVACY PROMISE:

Your audio recordings are 100% private and stored locally on your device. We do not upload, transfer, listen to, analyze, sell, or store your voice recordings or audio files on our servers or any cloud services. What you record stays strictly on your device.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Not Transmitted to Us)

When you use the App, the following content is created and stored exclusively on your device's local storage or a custom directory chosen by you:

  • Audio Recordings & Voice Memos: High-definition recordings created in AAC (M4A), AMR, 3GP, or WAV formats.
  • Recording Metadata: File names, recording duration, timestamps, sample rates, bitrates, file sizes, and audio source selections.
  • User Annotations & Bookmarks: Custom markers, tags, timestamps, labels (e.g., questions, highlights), and textual notes attached to recordings.
  • Categories & Organization: Tags such as Meetings, Lectures, Voice Notes, Music, Personal, or Favorites.
  • App Preferences: UI theme (dark/light), default recording format, sample rate, silence detection sensitivity, mic gain preferences, and language selection.

None of the above data is ever collected by Evera Apps, nor is it uploaded to our servers.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

While we do not collect personal identification information (such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number), the App integrates reputable third-party SDKs to provide advertising, diagnostics, and app performance analytics. These services may collect technical device data:

  1. Device & Network Identifiers:
    • Mobile Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID / AAID)
    • IP address (used to infer approximate coarse location such as country or city)
    • Android OS version, device manufacturer, device model, and hardware architecture
    • Network type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G) and carrier information
  2. App Usage & Diagnostic Telemetry:
    • Screen views, feature interactions (e.g., clicking Settings, unlocking features), session duration
    • Crash logs, stack traces, and system state at time of crash (via Firebase Crashlytics)
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver push notifications and service announcements

3. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

To record audio and provide scheduled recording features, the App requests specific Android runtime permissions:

PermissionTechnical NamePurpose & Usage
MicrophoneRECORD_AUDIOEssential. Required to record voice notes, lectures, meetings, and sounds. Audio input is captured and written directly to your local storage.
Audio SettingsMODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGSUsed to adjust input sensitivity (mic gain 0.5x–2.0x), enable hardware noise suppression, and configure audio source routing.
Bluetooth / Nearby DevicesBLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_ADMINRequired on Android 12+ to detect, connect, and route audio input from wireless Bluetooth headsets and external microphones.
Exact AlarmsSCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARMRequired to trigger scheduled recordings at the precise time specified by the user.
Post NotificationsPOST_NOTIFICATIONSRequired on Android 13+ to display ongoing recording controls (pause, resume, bookmark, stop) in the status bar and notify you before scheduled recordings.
Foreground ServiceFOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONEKeeps audio recording active without interruption when the screen is locked, turned off, or while switching apps.
Wake LockWAKE_LOCKPrevents the device CPU from going to sleep while actively recording audio or executing a scheduled recording.
Boot CompletedRECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDAutomatically reschedules your active scheduled recording timers if your device is restarted or rebooted.
Storage / SAFStorage Access FrameworkAllows you to select custom folders on your device or SD card to organize and save audio files.

You can revoke any granted permission at any time through your device's Android Settings > Apps > Voice Recorder > Permissions. Revoking microphone access will disable the ability to record audio.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request details about the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected through our third-party SDK partners over the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You can delete all local data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. You may also request deletion of telemetry data collected by third parties.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell your personal information or audio recordings for monetary consideration. However, using ad networks for targeted advertising may be deemed "sharing" or "selling" under California law. You can opt out via your device's ad tracking settings or through in-app consent preferences.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise your California privacy rights, contact us at appsofnirvana@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Uninstalling the App will remove recordings stored in the default app-specific directory (recordings saved in custom SAF external folders will remain on your device storage).
  • Analytics and Crash Data: Data collected by Firebase and AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's data retention schedules (typically aggregated and retained for 2 to 14 months).

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data:

  • Network communications between the App and third-party SDKs (AdMob, Firebase) occur strictly over encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • We advise users to maintain device-level security (PIN, pattern, biometrics) to protect locally stored recordings from unauthorized physical access.
