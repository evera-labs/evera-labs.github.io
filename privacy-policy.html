Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Evera Labs ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website or contact us as a developer. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked on its Google Play listing, which explains how that app handles data.

Information we collect

This website does not have accounts, sign-up forms, comments or tracking cookies, and we do not knowingly collect personal information through it. We collect personal information only when you choose to send it to us, for example by emailing us your name, email address and message.

How we use it

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, to provide support, and to improve our apps. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: our hosting provider may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts: this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google Play: links on this site open our apps on Google Play, which is run by Google and has its own privacy policy.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites or services.

Data retention

We keep emails only as long as needed to answer your request and handle any follow-up, and then delete them unless we are required to keep them by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
